Common Questions

Let's address what you're probably wondering.

How long is each session?

60 minutes. Enough time to go deep without feeling rushed, but structured enough to stay focused.

Is this therapy or coaching?

Coaching. We focus on moving forward, not unpacking trauma. If you need therapy, I'll be honest about that and can suggest resources.

Do I have to commit to multiple sessions?

No. Start with one. If it helps, we can continue. If not, no hard feelings. You're in control of your journey.

What if I'm not sure what I need help with?

That's exactly why you should book a session. Half the battle is getting clarity on what's actually going on. We'll figure it out together.

Is this available in Arabic and English?

Yes. Sessions can be conducted in either language, or a mix of both if that's more comfortable.

What happens after the 10 founding client spots are filled?

Regular pricing will apply. If you book now as a founding client, you'll have access to discounted rates for future sessions.
