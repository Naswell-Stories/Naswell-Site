## 🎨 Design Principles Applied:

1. **Visual First:** Images lead, text supports
2. **Scannable:** 3-5 words per line max
3. **Breathing Room:** White space between sections
4. **Mobile Priority:** Designed for phone first
5. **Emotion:** Images create feeling before reading
